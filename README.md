# 🚀 Why TenderMind AI?

Government procurement is information-heavy, document-intensive and highly
dependent on accurate compliance verification.

Procurement teams may need to evaluate:
- Udyam/MSME registration
- GST registration and return filing
- PAN and Income Tax compliance
- MCA21 company information
- Startup India and NSIC registration
- EPFO and ESIC compliance
- Make in India / local content requirements
- OEM authorization
- DigiLocker and document verification
- Blacklisting / debarment checks
- Tender-specific eligibility requirements
- Technical and financial requirements

TenderMind AI converts this complex information into structured,
evidence-backed procurement intelligence for faster and more consistent
compliance verification.

# 📊 Vendor Performance & Reliability Score (VPRS)

TenderMind AI introduces **Vendor Performance & Reliability Score (VPRS)** to enable transparent and evidence-based procurement.

The score considers:

- 📈 Previous Project Performance
- 📜 Compliance History
- 📑 Certificate Validity
- 💰 Financial Stability
- 🏢 Company Reliability
- 🛠️ Technical Qualification
- 📊 Historical Procurement Performance
- 🚨 Risk Indicators

VPRS provides an evidence-based view of vendor reliability using available
historical and verified procurement information.

# 👨‍💼 Human Decision Authority

TenderMind AI provides verification, evidence and decision support.

It does not autonomously:

- Qualify a bidder
- Disqualify a bidder
- Award a contract
- Change tender eligibility rules
- Blacklist a vendor
- Declare fraud

The final qualification or disqualification decision remains with the
authorized Procurement Officer.
